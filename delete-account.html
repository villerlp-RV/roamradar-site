Delete your RoamRadar account

RoamRadar by SKC Software Solutions. You can delete your account and the data stored with it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app

Open RoamRadar and go to Account → Help Center → Delete Account, type DELETE and confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

By email

Email help@silverkey.app from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my RoamRadar account". We'll confirm by email and delete the account and its data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (email, or the Google sign-in link)
  • Saved parks, saved searches and price alerts
  • Reservations and trips, including packing lists, fuel logs and travel plans
  • Your RV profile, maintenance schedule, repair log and photos
  • Pre-drive and departure checklists, emergency contacts and notes
  • Missing-park reports, your mailing-list subscription and usage history

We keep no copy of this data afterwards.

What is kept, and by whom

  • Stripe keeps its own record of any payment you made for a reservation, as the law requires. RoamRadar never stores card numbers.
  • A park you booked with keeps the booking details it received (name, dates, RV details).
  • If you claimed a park as its owner, the claim stays with that park's listing.
