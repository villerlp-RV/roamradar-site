Find your next RV spot

RoamRadar is a campground finder and RV trip planner for motorhomes, fifth wheels, travel trailers and camper vans. Find RV parks near you, compare them on a map, check availability for your dates, and keep your rig road-ready.

Find parks near you

Search by city, state or zip, or around your location. Filter by hookups, pull-throughs, RV length, pets and price.

Compare and book

Photos, rates, amenities and cancellation policies side by side. Reserve at parks that book through RoamRadar, or call direct.

Plan the trip

Saved parks with price history, packing lists, fuel tracking, departure checklists and bridge-safe routing.

Look after your rig

Maintenance schedules, repair logs, service history PDFs and NHTSA recall checks for your RV.

Every feature is free to use; you only pay for campsites you book. Open-spot counts are estimated from typical demand and RoamRadar bookings, not a live feed from the park office, so confirm with the park before you roll.
