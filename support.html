Support

Contact us

Email support@theroamradar.com. Include your account email, and your booking reference if it's about a reservation.

In the app

  • Tap the chat bubble on any screen to ask the RoamRadar assistant.
  • Account → Help Center has answers to common questions about searching, reservations, pricing and your account.
  • Use the Feedback page to send suggestions or report a problem.

Reservations and refunds

Each park sets its own cancellation policy, shown before you book. If a park cancels, you get a full refund. For billing questions, email us with your booking reference.
